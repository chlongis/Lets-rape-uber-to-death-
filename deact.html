In Minnesota, rideshare companies have to give a written notice with the reason for a deactivation and let you appeal (Minn. Stat. § 181C.04, in effect since late 2024). That law covers rideshare apps like Uber and Lyft, not food delivery. If you drive rideshare in Minnesota and got no reason, the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry takes complaints.
