How it works

Three steps, then you own it

STEP 01

Build it once

Your trips and deliveries, your rating, your car, your area. Attach a screenshot of your stats screen and the profile shows as verified. Takes about two minutes.

STEP 02

Hand it out

A permanent link, a QR code for the seatback, a printable card, or save straight into a rider's or customer's contacts. However you want to give it out, it's there.

STEP 03

Get found directly

Riders and customers look you up and reach you themselves. You set the price, you keep all of it, and nobody takes a percentage for making the introduction.

Why this matters

Your reputation is the product they're selling

Uber's and DoorDash's fee isn't payment for an app. It's payment for being the only one who knows you're good at this.

It doesn't transfer

Four years of five-star ratings is worth nothing the moment you open a different app. That isn't a technical limitation — it's the entire retention strategy.

It vanishes on deactivation

An algorithm decides, there's often no appeal, and everything you built is gone with it. A record you hold a copy of can't be switched off.

It costs you 30% forever

As long as they're the only place your history exists, the cut is whatever they say it is. A rider or customer who can find you directly changes that number to zero.

Consumers pay the price too

Literally. Delivery fees have gotten crazy high over the years and consumers have suffered while drivers don't see any of it

On shift right now? Check the offer first.

The app shows the payout, not what the trip costs your car or the empty miles back. Punch in the offer and get Take it, Maybe or Skip, based on your own car and your own floor, before the timer runs out.
