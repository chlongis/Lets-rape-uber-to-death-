Why tho

Four years of five stars means jack shit the second you open a different app. That's on purpose. It keeps you stuck.

One bad day with the algorithm and you're deactivated, usually with no real appeal, and everything you built is just gone.

And the whole time they're taking a fat cut for being the only ones who know you're good at this. If a customer can find you directly, that cut goes to zero.

Customers are getting fucked too, by the way. Fees go up every year and drivers sure as hell aren't the ones seeing that money.

Is that shit even legal??

In Minnesota, Uber and Lyft drivers have a pay floor: $1.28 a mile, 31 cents a minute, $5 minimum a trip. Delivery drivers? Nothing. Zero. That's why DoorDash can legally send you some $3-for-12-miles bullshit here. Seattle and NYC actually have one for delivery.
