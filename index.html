How it works

1

Make your page

Rating, trips, car, area. Takes two minutes. Throw in a screenshot of your stats screen and it shows as verified.

2

Give it out

A link, a QR code for the back of the seat, a card you hand out, or straight into someone's contacts. Whatever works.

3

Keep the money

People reach you directly. You set the price and you keep all of it. Nobody takes a cut for an introduction they didn't make.

Your rating is their leash

The fee they take is for being the only ones who know you're good at this.

It doesn't transfer

Four years of five stars means jack shit the second you open a different app. That's on purpose. It keeps you stuck.

One switch and it's gone

An algorithm decides, there's often no real appeal, and everything you built goes with it. A record you own can't get switched off.

So they set the cut

As long as your history only exists in their app, they take whatever cut they feel like. A customer who can find you directly takes that to zero.

Customers get screwed too

Delivery fees keep climbing every year and drivers aren't the ones seeing that money.

On shift? Check it before you hit accept.

The app shows you the payout. It doesn't show you the gas, the wear on your car, or the empty miles home. Screenshot the offer or punch it in and get Take it, Maybe or Skip, based on your car and your floor.

Did they even pay you legally?

Minnesota has a pay floor for rideshare. Seattle and New York City have one for delivery. Minnesota delivery drivers don't have one at all. Run a pay period through it and see if you got shorted, and who to file with if you did.
