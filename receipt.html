Where did the money go?

One order, three numbers: what the customer paid, what the food actually costs at the counter, and what the driver got. The rest went somewhere. Make a card and post it.

The order

Use real numbers off a real receipt and pay screen. If you only know some of them, fill in what you can and the card will say what's missing.

Keep it bulletproof

  • Only post numbers you can show. Keep the receipt and the pay screenshot.
  • "The app kept" includes what the app paid the restaurant on top of the counter price, if anything, plus card fees. The card says so.
  • Post it as one real order, not as an average.
  • Black out names, addresses and order numbers before posting a screenshot next to it.
